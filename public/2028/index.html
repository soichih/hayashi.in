How to read this

What the evidence tags mean
  • 2026 proposal: something the candidate has proposed or said this year, the closest thing to a 2028 platform.
  • Record: votes, laws signed, or actions taken in office.
  • Past campaign: positions from a 2020 or 2024 run, which may have shifted since.
  • No clear position: nothing specific found in public statements. Treat it as unknown, not as opposition.
Where the numbers come from

Nomination and presidency odds: PredictMarketCap's Polymarket/Kalshi comparison, averaged across both venues. Polls: Morning Consult, Aug 28–30, 2026 (via Newsweek), and Emerson College, July 2026, where a candidate wasn't listed by Morning Consult. Polls this early swing widely and mostly measure name recognition.

Likelihood of running

This is a judgment call from public statements (for example, Vance saying he'll decide after the midterms, Harris saying she's "thinking" about it, Ossoff saying he has no interest, Rubio saying he'd back Vance). It will move after the November 2026 midterms, when announcements typically start.
